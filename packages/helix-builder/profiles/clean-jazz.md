### Snapshot Profile 1: "Clean / Jazz"
> **Core Objective:** Provide an ultra-clean, pristine canvas with high headroom for complex jazz chording or glassy funk rhythm playing. This patch focuses on dynamic string tracking and clear, spacious stereo imaging.

#### 1. Signal Chain Layout & Block States
 * **Input Block:** Guitar Input • Multi-purpose Noise Gate **[BYPASSED]** *(Turned off to allow light, soft jazz fingerpicking to ring out completely without getting cut off).*
 * **Block 1 (Drive):** Minotaur Overdrive **[BYPASSED]**
 * **Block 2 (Amp):** US Deluxe Vib **[ENABLED]**
 * **Block 3 (Cab):** 1x12 Deluxe (Open Back) **[ENABLED]**
 * **Block 4 (EQ):** Parametric EQ **[ENABLED]**
 * **Block 5 (Modulation):** Deluxe Chorus **[ENABLED / OPTIONAL]**
 * **Block 6 (Delay):** Simple Delay **[BYPASSED]**
 * **Block 7 (Reverb):** Dynamic Hall **[ENABLED - Medium Mix]**
 * **Output Block:** Multi-routing Main Out
