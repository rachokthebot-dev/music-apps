### Snapshot Profile: "Rock Rhythm / Crunch"
> **Core Objective:** Deliver an aggressive, mid-forward classic/hard rock crunch with seamless spillover transitions. The gain structure relies on an optimized single-amp model rather than running dual-amp paths, maximizing DSP and keeping the low-end punch focused.

#### 1. Signal Chain Layout & Block States
This snapshot utilizes a single-entry signal path split into a series-parallel structure post-cabinet to allow dry note clarity to co-exist with ambient spillover.
 * **Input Block:** Guitar Input • Multi-purpose Noise Gate **[ENABLED]**
 * **Block 1 (Drive):** Minotaur or Scream 808 **[ENABLED]**
 * **Block 2 (Amp):** Placater Dirty or Revv Gen Red **[ENABLED]**
 * **Block 3 (Cab/IR):** 4x12 Greenback 25 or Dual IR Block **[ENABLED]**
 * **Block 4 (EQ):** 10-Band Graphic or Parametric EQ **[ENABLED]**
 * **Block 5 (Modulation):** Script Phase or Deluxe Chorus **[BYPASSED]**
 * **Block 6 (Delay):** Transistor Tape or Simple Delay **[ENABLED - Low Mix]**
 * **Block 7 (Reverb):** Dynamic Hall or Plate **[ENABLED - Low Mix]**
 * **Output Block:** Multi-routing Main XLR/1/4" Out
